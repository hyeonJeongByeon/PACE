Four things patients can do in a visit

Take a moment with these — you'll practice them in a role-play built around your own topic.

P
Present — telling your story: when it started, how bad it gets, what you've tried, what you hope for.
"It started six weeks ago, and ibuprofen barely touches it."
A
Ask — getting your questions answered.
"What's that test for, and how will the result change things?"
C
Check — making sure you understood.
"So just to check — twice a day with food, for two weeks?"
E
Express — saying what's worrying you.
"Honestly, I'm worried it's something more serious."
